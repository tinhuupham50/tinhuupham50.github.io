Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-23

This site hosts an OAuth 2.0 prototype application ("ocamlfuse Google Drive OAuth") used solely to mount the account owner's personal Google Drive as a local filesystem using the google-drive-ocamlfuse tool.

  • Used only by the account owner (no third-party users).
  • Does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.
  • Requests access only to its owner's Google Drive.
  • Stores OAuth credentials locally on the owner's device.
